Humanoid robotics — Unitree G1 (2026)

Teaching a humanoid to jump with reinforcement learning

Personal R&D · final-year internship, KUFUSHA (Japan)

Custom Isaac Lab environment trained with PPO (RSL-RL) to make the G1 track a reference jump.

  • Six-phase reward machine (idle, crouch, take-off, flight, landing, stand) with per-phase weights, reference-state initialization, early terminations, per-joint action scaling.
  • Training plateaued: I traced it to a conflict between the tracking reward and the termination penalty. After the fix, the policy tracked the reference with zero falls.
  • Validated Sim2Sim in MuJoCo, exported to ONNX.
